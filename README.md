# YJ-MoLi

Machine learning for environmental systems · Computer vision · Robotics

[中文](README.zh-CN.md) · [Academic homepage](https://blog.dengshu.cloud/) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=emqGrgMAAAAJ) · [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0009-0007-5488-3557)

I am a master's student in Environmental Science and Engineering at Guangxi Normal University. My current interests concern machine learning for environmental data, predictive modelling, and model interpretation, including drinking-water treatment. My undergraduate work in Internet of Things Engineering at Taiyuan University focused on robotic perception and manipulation, computer vision, and AI-assisted diagnosis.

The [academic homepage](https://blog.dengshu.cloud/) contains the detailed research record and timeline; the [public repository directory](PROJECTS.md) separates research implementations, application and competition projects, upstream resources, and archives.
